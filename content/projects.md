# Selected projects

## BoardSync - collaborative task organization

A Kanban project exploring columns, cards, assignment, and shared work.
Workspace screenshots show the board and card-details interface.

- Status: in progress.
- Preview: https://boardsync-web.netlify.app/
- Stack direction: React/Next.js, .NET, PostgreSQL, SignalR, Redis.
- Story: /blog/boardsync-making-work-visible

## StoreCraft - merchant workspace and storefront builder

An experimental e-commerce MVP informed by BigCommerce storefront experience.
Products, orders, shipping settings, themes, and page design connect the merchant
workspace with a customer storefront.

- Preview: https://storecraft-demo.netlify.app/
- Stack: Next.js, TypeScript, .NET, PostgreSQL, Supabase.
- Public sandbox: fictional data, browser changes, and simulated payments.
- The hosted authenticated merchant journey still needs verification.
- Story: /blog/storecraft-from-storefronts-to-store-builder

## OrderPilot - the work after checkout

A seeded OMS prototype for location assignment, picking, packing, shipping, and exceptions.
- Preview: https://orderpilot-demo.onrender.com/
- Stack: Next.js, TypeScript, ASP.NET Core, SQLite.
- Story: /blog/orderpilot-the-work-after-checkout
- Public sample login; hosted demo state resets on redeploy or instance replacement.
- StoreCraft integration remains future work.

## K3 Billing Tracker - a friend's business workflow

Built for a friend's WiFi business to address payment-tracking struggles.
- Preview: https://k3-billing-tracker.netlify.app/ — choose View demo.
- Stack: Next.js, TypeScript, .NET, PostgreSQL.
- Story: /blog/k3-billing-tracker-built-for-a-friend
- Subscribers, daily/weekly/monthly renewals, payment history, and mobile layouts.
- Public read-only demo uses sample customers and payments; owner records stay private.
- Records payments without charging customers.

No live payment processing, measured commercial outcomes, or connected OMS are claimed.
